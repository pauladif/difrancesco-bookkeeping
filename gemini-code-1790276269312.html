About Me

Experienced Financial Professional

Hello, my name is Paula DiFrancesco. Located in Cleveland, Ohio, I am a professional accountant with over 10 years of experience dedicated to helping small businesses and non-profit organizations maintain financial health and growth.

Over the years, I have gained extensive experience working with commercial banks, large corporations, small businesses, and non-profit organizations across the United States.

Whether you are an established enterprise or a growing local venture, I combine structured, corporate-level rigor with personalized, dependable support to give you complete peace of mind over your financials.
